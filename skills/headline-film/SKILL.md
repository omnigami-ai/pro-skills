# Headline film (listing)

This is the listing of an omnigami Pro skill. The skill itself is in a private repository that buyers get access to;
this folder holds no steps or code, so don't try to make anything from it.

**What it makes:** A launch film with big type, real app cards and a beat to follow. Headlines step around your real app screens. Letters briefly switch to lookalikes, cards turn and grow into close views of your product working, and quick montages lead to your mark and closing line. You get a wide MP4 with music and an editable film.

**Where to get it:** https://omnigami.ai/skills/headline-film ($4.99, once).
